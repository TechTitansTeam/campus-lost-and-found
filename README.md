# 🎓 Campus Lost and Found

### Find What's Lost. Return What's Found. Build a Better Campus.

## 🌟 About the Project

**Campus Lost and Found** is a smart campus platform designed to help students, faculty members, and other campus users report lost belongings and find items that have been misplaced or discovered within the college campus.

Losing personal belongings such as ID cards, wallets, books, mobile phones, keys, and other essential items can be stressful. Finding the rightful owner of a misplaced item can also be difficult without a centralized reporting system.

Our project aims to simplify this process by providing a common digital platform where users can share lost-and-found information, search for matching items, and make campus life more convenient and organized.

This repository contains the prototype of Campus Lost and Found, demonstrating the proposed interface, design, and user workflow.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

In colleges and educational institutions, students frequently lose personal belongings in classrooms, laboratories, libraries, cafeterias, and other campus locations. Currently, finding these items often depends on word of mouth, class groups, notices, or personally contacting other students.

These methods can make it difficult to connect the person who lost an item with the person who found it.

Campus Lost and Found addresses this challenge by proposing a centralized platform for reporting, discovering, and recovering lost belongings.

## 💡 Our Solution

Campus Lost and Found provides a simple and accessible digital space for users to report missing items and share information about items they have found.

The platform is designed to make it easier to:

- Report lost belongings with relevant details.
- Publish information about found items.
- Browse and search available reports.
- Compare item descriptions and locations to identify possible matches.
- Connect item owners with finders through available contact options.
- Support a more organized and responsible campus community.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. **Report a Lost Item:** A user provides the available details of a missing belonging.
2. **Report a Found Item:** A person who discovers an item shares its details on the platform.
3. **Search for Items:** Users browse the available reports and search for relevant information.
4. **Identify a Possible Match:** Item descriptions and locations can help users recognize a matching belonging.
5. **Recover the Item:** The owner and finder can follow the available contact and recovery process.

**Example:** A student loses an ID card in the college library. Another student finds it and reports it on the platform. The owner can check the found-item listings and follow the available process to recover the ID card.

This describes the intended workflow; actual report storage, matching, and communication depend on the features implemented in the prototype.
